## Three promises

1. **Código que puedes leer** — every tool ships with readable open-source code (AGPLv3).
2. **Métodos publicados** — every number has a source, a date, and stated assumptions.
3. **Sin humo** — we document what our tools cannot do (e.g., "no order-flow institucional") as clearly as what they do.
